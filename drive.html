Questions

How does the $75 weekly cap work?

Every week, 20% of each fare goes to Cavalier until the total reaches $75. After that you keep 100% of your fares for the rest of the week. If 20% of your fares comes to less than $75, you only pay that 20%. The count starts over each week.

When does Cavalier launch?

We'll launch on a set date once the rider waitlist is ready. Everyone who signs up will hear about it first by text and email.

Where will I drive?

Albany first. We're starting in one dense area so riders get quick pickups and drivers stay busy, then growing from there.

How do payouts work?

Payouts will be weekly. Full details will be shared before launch.

Is this a job?

No. Cavalier drivers will be independent drivers, not employees on a schedule. The full driver terms will be shared before launch.
